❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What Industries do you serve?

We serve a wide range of industries including Healthcare, Finance & Banking, Retail & E-Commerce, Education, Logistics, Real Estate, Manufacturing, and Government sectors.


Q: How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary depending on complexity. A simple website may take 2–4 weeks, while a complex enterprise application can take 3–12 months. We provide detailed timelines after a thorough requirements analysis.


Q: Do you offer post-launch support and maintenance?

Yes! We offer comprehensive post-launch support packages including 24/7 monitoring, bug fixes, performance optimization, security updates, and feature enhancements.


Q: Is my data secure with TechNova?

Absolutely. We follow industry best practices for data security including end-to-end encryption, GDPR compliance, regular security audits, and penetration testing. We are ISO 27001 certified for information security management.


Q: Do you work with startups or only large enterprises?

We work with businesses of all sizes — from early-stage startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Our flexible pricing and engagement models are designed to accommodate varying budgets and needs.


Q: Can I see a demo before committing?

Yes! We offer free discovery calls and product demos. Contact our sales team to schedule a personalized walkthrough of our solutions tailored to your business requirements.
